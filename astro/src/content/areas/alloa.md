## Joiners and builders in Alloa and Tullibody

Alloa is about eight miles east of Stirling along the A907, and Tullibody is on the way. Both are well inside our normal working area, so you get the same free site visit and written quote as a customer in Stirling. We also take on work in the Hillfoots villages, so ask when you enquire.

## Alloa and Tullibody homes and what they need

- **Victorian sandstone terraces and villas** in Alloa's town centre and the older streets around it: sash and case windows, period doors and mouldings, staircases, and [kitchens](/services/kitchens/) that fit older layouts.
- **Interwar and post-war housing** across both towns, where kitchens, flooring, doors and built-in storage make up most of the work, along with [loft conversions](/services/loft-conversions/) where the roof allows.
- **Modern estates** around Tullibody and the edges of Alloa: kitchen upgrades, garden rooms and sheds, [extensions](/services/extensions/) and finishing [joinery](/services/general-joinery/).

## Planning and building warrants in Clackmannanshire

Alloa and Tullibody are in the Clackmannanshire Council area, not Stirling, so planning applications and building warrants go to Clackmannanshire Council. The process is the same in practice: extensions and loft conversions need a building warrant, and we can guide you through it or work alongside your architect.
