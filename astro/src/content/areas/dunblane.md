## Joiners and builders in Dunblane

Dunblane is a short run up the A9 from our Stirling base, so a site visit is easy to fit in and there is no travel premium on the quote. It is one of the areas we cover most often.

## Dunblane homes and what they need

- **The old town around the Cathedral.** Stone-built Victorian and Edwardian houses, several within the Dunblane conservation area. Typical jobs are sash and case window repairs and replacements, period doors and mouldings, staircase work and [kitchens](/services/kitchens/) designed to suit an older house.
- **Bungalows and 1960s to 1980s family homes.** Dunblane has a lot of these, and their roof space is often ideal for a [loft conversion](/services/loft-conversions/). Rear and side [extensions](/services/extensions/) are the other common request, usually to open up a kitchen-diner.
- **Newer estates on the edge of town.** Kitchen upgrades, built-in wardrobes and storage, garden rooms, flooring and finishing [joinery](/services/general-joinery/).

## Planning and building warrants

Dunblane sits within the Stirling Council area, so planning applications and building warrants go to Stirling Council. Conservation area rules apply in the old town, which can affect window styles, external materials and what counts as permitted development. We will tell you at the quote stage if your project is likely to need a warrant or planning consent, and can bring in an architect or engineer where drawings are required.
