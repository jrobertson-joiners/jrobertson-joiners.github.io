## Joiners and builders in Bridge of Allan

Bridge of Allan is only a few minutes from Stirling and is a regular part of our patch. The town grew as a Victorian spa resort, and much of its housing dates from that period, which makes it a good fit for a joinery-led contractor.

## Bridge of Allan homes and what they need

- **Victorian villas and terraces.** Large stone villas on the hillside above Henderson Street and sandstone terraces in the town centre, much of it within the Bridge of Allan conservation area. This is where our period joinery gets used most: sash and case windows, shutters, panelled doors, staircases and mouldings made to match the originals, as well as full [kitchen](/services/kitchens/) and bathroom refits that respect the house.
- **Later 20th-century houses.** Semis, bungalows and detached homes on the edges of the town, where [loft conversions](/services/loft-conversions/) and [extensions](/services/extensions/) are the common request.
- **Flats and rental properties.** With the University of Stirling next door there is a steady demand for kitchen replacements, flooring, doors and [repairs](/services/general-joinery/) turned around quickly between tenancies.

## Planning and building warrants

Bridge of Allan is in the Stirling Council area for both planning and building standards. The conservation area covers much of the older part of town, which affects external work such as windows, doors and extensions. Extensions and loft conversions need a building warrant. We handle this routinely and will flag it at the quote stage.
